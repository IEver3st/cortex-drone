# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-01)

### Added
- First public release.
- Camera, Police and FPV drones you toss up from your inventory and fly through a stabilised gimbal camera.
- Cine, Normal and Sport flight modes, plus Acro on every drone in two styles: Glide and Manual.
- Cinematic mode with automatic Track, Follow, Orbit, Dronie, Rocket and Helix shots, and Film me.
- Police tools: night and thermal vision, 16x zoom, target lock and follow, plate and ID lookup, and a taser.
- Per-drone batteries that drain and recharge, and battery swaps by hand.
- Signal range, return-to-home, no-fly zones and obstacle sensing.
- Standalone, Qbox or qb-core, with ox_inventory support.
